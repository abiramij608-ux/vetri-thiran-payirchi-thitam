(The Landlord and Tenant may individually be referred to as a **"Party"** and collectively as the **"Parties"**.)

---

### 2. DEMISED PREMISES
The Landlord agrees to let out, and the Tenant agrees to take on rent, the property situated at **[RENTAL PROPERTY ADDRESS]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Premises"**).

---

### 3. TERM OF TENANCY
* **Duration:** The tenancy shall be for a fixed term of **eleven (11) months**, commencing on **October 1, 2026**, and expiring on **[EXPIRATION DATE]**, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.
* **Renewal:** Any renewal or extension of this tenancy shall be subject to the mutual written consent of both Parties on mutually agreed terms.

---

### 4. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
* **Monthly Rent:** The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord a monthly rent of **Rs. 10,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand only)**.
* **Due Date:** The rent shall be payable in advance on or before the **5th day of every calendar month**.
* **Payment Mode:** Payment shall be made via **[MODE OF PAYMENT: e.g., Bank Transfer / Cash / Cheque]** to the Landlord's designated account/details: **[LANDLORD PAYMENT DETAILS]**.

---

### 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
* **Deposit Amount:** The Tenant has paid/agreed to pay a refundable security deposit of **Rs. 30,000/- (Rupees Thirty Thousand only)** to the Landlord upon or before the signing of this Agreement.
* **Refund Terms:** The security deposit shall be refunded to the Tenant, without interest, at the time of vacating and handing over peaceful, vacant possession of the Premises, subject to deductions for any unpaid rent, utility bills, or damages caused to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear.

---

### 6. MAINTENANCE AND USE OF PREMISES
* **Maintenance:** The Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good, clean, and tenantable condition throughout the tenancy.
* **Use:** The Premises shall be used solely for **[RESIDENTIAL / COMMERCIAL]** purposes and the Tenant shall not carry out any unlawful, illegal, or hazardous activities on the Premises.
* **Alterations:** The Tenant shall not make any structural additions or alterations to the Premises without obtaining prior written consent from the Landlord.

---

### 7. TERMINATION AND NOTICE
* Either Party may terminate this Agreement prior to the expiration of the term by providing **[NOTICE PERIOD, e.g., 1 month]** written notice to the other Party.
* Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, the Tenant shall peacefully vacate and hand over possession of the Premises to the Landlord in its original condition, subject to reasonable wear and tear.

---

### 8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the applicable laws of India, and any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts in **Tirupur, Tamil Nadu**.
